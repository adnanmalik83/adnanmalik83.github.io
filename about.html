ABOUT AI LABS

Building Practical AI for Real-World Problems

AILabs is an independent AI software initiative created by Adnan Malik, focused on building practical solutions across artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, fraud detection, analytics and automation.

THE CREATOR

Adnan Malik

Adnan Malik is an AI and telecommunications technology professional with more than two decades of experience in telecom revenue assurance, fraud management and technology systems.

His work has involved designing, implementing and leading fraud detection and revenue assurance solutions across telecommunications environments, with experience spanning Pakistan, Qatar, Bahrain and the United Kingdom.

AILabs represents the next stage of that experience: transforming practical industry knowledge into modern AI-powered software products that can be built, tested and improved in the real world.

OUR APPROACH

The goal is simple: take useful ideas, turn them into working software, test them in real scenarios and continuously improve them.
